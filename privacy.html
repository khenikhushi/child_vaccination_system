Welcome to the Child Vaccination System. We value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Personal data provided during registration (e.g., name, email, phone).
  • Child and parent details submitted for vaccination scheduling.
  • Vaccination records, location info, and appointment history.

How We Use the Data

  • To schedule and manage vaccination appointments.
  • To send reminders, confirmations, or follow-up messages.
  • To improve our services and ensure child health safety.

Data Protection

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no system is 100% secure, so we recommend safeguarding your login credentials.

Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies to improve user experience and analyze website traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Your Rights

  • Access and review your personal information.
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw consent to data processing where applicable.
