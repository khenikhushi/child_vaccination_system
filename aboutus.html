About Us

Smart & Timely Vaccinations for a Healthier Tomorrow

Child Vaccination System is a digital platform aimed at simplifying and improving vaccination tracking for children. Our system provides parents and healthcare workers with timely vaccination schedules, appointment booking, and easy record access.

Our Mission

To ensure no child misses a life-saving vaccination by providing an accessible, smart, and reliable vaccination tracking system.

What We Provide

  • Digital vaccination history for each child.
  • Anganwadi center integration for better coordination.
  • Doctor and parent dashboards.

Why It Matters

Vaccinations are vital to preventing childhood diseases. Our system helps bridge the gap between healthcare services and parents, ensuring timely and effective immunization through modern technology.
